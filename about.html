WHO WE ARE

A Comfortable Massage Experience

Ladies Care Massage offers massage service options for women who want a calm and comfortable experience. Our listed services include shoulder massage, back or kamar massage, neck and upper-body massage, and full-body relaxation massage.

We believe clear communication is important. Before booking, customers can ask about the service, starting price, session duration and available night-time appointments. Customers can also explain their preferred pressure and identify any areas they do not want included.

Our aim is to make the appointment process straightforward. Send us a WhatsApp message, tell us which service you are interested in, and wait for confirmation of availability and final pricing.

Massage is intended for relaxation and comfort and is not a replacement for medical care. If you have an injury, severe pain or a health concern that could affect massage, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional before booking.
